## Direction contract

THESIS: Rep One is a flat-pack assembly manual for your body, printed on one ultramarine flood. Every exercise is numbered steps, each drawn from the angle that shows it best. It refuses the category default of a dark gym hero with a neon accent and stock athlete photos.

OWN-WORLD: The page is the colour: ultramarine fills every page, with white line art and white type. Depth comes only from overlapping flat tints of the same blue, never from shading (raise from the Łowicz papercut). Giant expanded-black step numerals jump straight to small caption labels with no middle sizes (raise from Greiman). Mistakes are drawn as ✕ frames and correct cues as ✓, in the manual's own visual language. Primary buttons are liquid glass over the blue.

STORY: A beginner sees that form can be learned one numbered step at a time, believes it isn't intimidating, opens the catalogue, starts at No. 01 Seated Cable Row and steps 1→7.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The REP ONE wordmark sits top-left on the flood. On the left (7 of 12 columns) is a 32px-rounded "Fig. 1" demo panel with a white inset frame line. On the right, "See catalogue" is set at display scale with a one-line subhead and the glass "Browse exercises" button directly below. Behind everything are three flat parallax layers: giant outline numerals, cut-shape plates and cable stack, and white manual arrows.
